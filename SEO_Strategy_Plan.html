Keyword research methodology

5-Step Research Process

  • Interview sales, support & product teams for seed terms
  • Mine Google Search Console impressions & queries
  • Expand via Ahrefs, SEMrush & AnswerThePublic
  • Analyse top 5 competitor keyword profiles
  • Score by volume, difficulty & business relevance

Prioritisation Benchmarks

  • Target keywords with 500+ monthly searches
  • Keyword Difficulty <40 (new) / <60 (established)
  • Prefer SERP features: featured snippets & PAA
  • Quick wins: keywords ranking positions 5–20
  • Score business relevance 1–5 per keyword

Keyword mapping rules

  • Supporting keywords must share the same intent
  • Homepage targets highest-volume brand/category term
  • Blog pages target informational/question-based terms
  • Product pages target transactional commercial keywords

6 link acquisition tactics
✍

1. Guest Posting

Contribute expert articles to authoritative industry publications. Target 4–8 posts/month. Vary anchor text across branded, partial-match, and generic terms.

📣

2. Digital PR

Create link-worthy assets: original research, industry surveys, free tools, interactive infographics, and newsjacking expert opinion pieces.

🔗

3. Broken Link Building

Find broken outbound links in your niche via Ahrefs. Create a replacement resource and pitch site owners. Target 20+ opportunities per month.

🎙

4. HARO / Expert Quotes

Sign up to HARO, Qwoted & SourceBottle. Respond to 5–10 relevant journalist queries per week for high-authority media placements.

📚

5. Resource Pages

Search for "best [topic] resources" pages. Identify 30–50 well-maintained resource pages per quarter and pitch your most useful content.

🤝

6. Partnerships

Write vendor testimonials, co-author whitepapers and webinars with complementary brands. Participate in expert roundups and Q&A features.

Technical SEO Strategy

Core Web Vitals  ·  Architecture  ·  On-Page Elements  ·  Schema
Core Web Vitals — required thresholds
LCP · Largest Contentful Paint
< 2.5s

Optimise server response, images & critical CSS path. Use preload hints for hero images.

INP · Interaction to Next Paint
< 200ms

Minimise long JS tasks. Reduce main-thread blocking. Break up heavy event handlers.

CLS · Cumulative Layout Shift
< 0.1

Reserve image and ad dimensions. Avoid dynamic content insertion above the fold.

Speed optimisation actions
  • Enable Gzip or Brotli compression at server level
  • Implement CDN (Cloudflare / Fastly) across all assets
  • Convert all images to WebP; use lazy loading
  • Minify and defer non-critical JavaScript and CSS
  • Enable browser caching with cache-control headers
  • Use preconnect/preload for critical third-party resources
Site architecture rules
  • Flat structure — max 3 clicks from homepage to any page
  • Lowercase, hyphen-separated, keyword-rich URLs
  • Submit dynamic XML sitemap to Google Search Console
  • Block non-indexable pages in robots.txt — never CSS/JS
  • Every page linked from at least one other page
  • Self-referencing canonical tags on all pages
